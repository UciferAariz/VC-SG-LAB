Virtual Physics Lab — Vernier Callipers & Screw Gauge

Vernier Callipers Least count, zero error and readings of lengths, diameters and depths. Screw Gauge Pitch, least count, zero error and the diameter of a wire.

How to Use

  1. Explore: open an instrument. Drag the vernier slider, or drag / scroll on the thimble of the screw gauge. The Readout tab shows every value with a one-line explanation (ⓘ).
  2. Objects: pick a bob, cylinder, beaker or block (vernier), or a wire, coin, glass slide, ball or paper stack (screw gauge). Their true sizes are hidden. Use Rotate / reposition between readings.
  3. Practice: the readout is hidden. Enter LC, zero error, the scale readings and the corrected reading; every step is checked and common mistakes get a targeted hint.
  4. Experiment: record a full observation table, get the error analysis and the derived quantity, then download a CSV or print an A4 practical record.
  5. Presentation: press P on an instrument page for a high-contrast, step-by-step demo. A USB clicker works: Space / → / PageDown next, Backspace / ← / PageUp back.
  6. Whole class, same data: in Settings, everyone types the same session seed to get identical objects and zero errors.

Keyboard

  • Vernier (click the instrument first): ← → 0.01 mm, Shift 1 mm, Alt 0.001 mm.
  • Screw gauge: ↑ ↓ one circular division, Shift 0.1 division, PageUp / PageDown one turn, R ratchet, O open.
  • Both: L lock, M magnifier, + − zoom, F fit, S focus on the scale, 0 reset view, P presentation.

About

An interactive, physically exact virtual lab for Class 11/12 (CBSE / NCERT, NEET / JEE level). Every reading is computed from the instrument's geometry: the scales are drawn exactly as they would look at the true position, and the reading is what a careful student would record. Zero-error signs follow NCERT: corrected reading = observed reading − zero error.

It runs entirely on this computer, with no internet connection, no accounts, no tracking and no cookies.
